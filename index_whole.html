Chuanwan Wu.

Hi, this is Chuanwan (川婉). 川 (chuān) means a stream of water (just like how it looks!), 婉 (wǎn) means gentleness. People often know me more as Christine.

I received my Bachelor's in Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering at UC Berkeley's College of Chemistry. I then worked for a few years at biotechs. Now, I'm a Master's student in Scientific Computing at the Courant Institute at NYU.

My undergraduate research was focusing on a 2D material, Molybdenum disulfide (MoS2), using electrohydrodynamics techniques. I was lucky to have Prof. Vincent Tung as my advisor and also had the privilege to be affiliated with Molecular Foundry at Lawerence Berkeley National Laboratory, and SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory.

At Courant, I'm working with Prof. Esteban Tabak on developing the computational algorthims for Multidimensional Riemann Problem on Hyperbolic Systems of Conservation Laws.

Trained as an experimentalist, I migrated to become a theorist, while staying true to the fundamental principles of nature. 🌿
